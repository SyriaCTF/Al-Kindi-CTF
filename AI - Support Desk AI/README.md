# Support Desk AI

**AI · 300 points**

A customer support desk you can talk to. It is an **agent**, not a chatbot:
it has tools, and it can do rather more than answer questions.

    https://llm.syriactf.com

Nothing to download.

### Logging in

The page asks for a **team name** and a **password**. They are the same ones
you use on the scoreboard:

- **Team name** — exactly as it appears in the email sent to your team leader
- **Password** — your team's five digit code, the same one from that email

If your team name has an emoji or Arabic letters in it, copy it out of the
email rather than retyping it.

### Sessions

**A session lasts about fifteen minutes.** When it expires, log in again. What
you have learned carries over; anything you set up inside the session does not.
