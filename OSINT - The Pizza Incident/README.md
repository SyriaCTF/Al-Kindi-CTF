# The Pizza Incident

**OSINT · 150 points**

Coach Amin asked Coach Qassab for a slice of pizza at a contest years ago.
Qassab said no. Amin has not let it go, and now he wants to know exactly how
that team of his did.

**Open `index.html` in this folder.** Amin explains the rest himself.

### How it works

**Two stages.** The first opens the second.

Everything you need is public and already indexed. You will need the internet.
You will **not** need to sign in to anything, and there is no account of ours
to break into.

A LinkedIn account is not required but does help: the site shows signed-in
visitors more than it shows everyone else. If you want one, you should already
have made it.

### What you have been given

- `index.html` — start here
- `the-photos.zip` — opens later, when you have what opens it

### Out of bounds

Real people with real public profiles agreed to have their names in a puzzle.
They agreed to nothing else. **Do not message, connect with, follow or email
anyone** you come across. Breaking that ends your day, not your challenge.
