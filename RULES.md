# Rules

Read this before you open anything else. One of these you need to sort out
before the event starts, not during it.

## While you are working

**Do it yourselves. AI included, with one exception.**
No outside help, and that means AI assistants. Asking one what a file format
is, or what some technique means, is studying. Handing it a challenge file and
taking back the answer is not, and it is the one thing that makes the whole day
pointless.

The exception is **Eden**, at `bin.syriactf.com`. Use whatever you like on that
one, AI included, as much as you want.

**Be ready to explain how you solved something.**
We will ask. We will start with the teams at the top. If you cannot explain how
you got a flag, you do not keep the points. Assume this will happen rather than
hoping it will not.

**Keep flags to yourselves.**
Not between teams, not in a group chat, not after you finish. If a flag turns
up somewhere it should not, both teams involved are out.

## Look, do not touch

Some of this event involves real people and their real public profiles. They
agreed to have their names in a puzzle. They did not agree to anything else.

**Do not message, connect with, follow, email, or contact anyone** you come
across while solving. Do not try to log in as anyone. Do not go near an account
that is not part of the trail you were pointed at.

Same goes for the infrastructure. The scoring site, the venue network, and
other teams' laptops are all out of scope. Attack the challenges, nothing else.

Breaking this one is not a warning, it is the end of your day.

## Sessions on the hosted challenges

**Eden (`bin.syriactf.com`) and Support Desk AI (`llm.syriactf.com`) hand you a
session that lasts about fifteen minutes.** When it runs out you start a fresh
one. Nothing you have learned is lost, but anything you had set up inside it
is, so do not leave a long chain of steps until the last minute of a session.

For Eden you can skip the clock entirely: run it locally in Docker, work out
your solution there with no timer, and come back to the live host only when you
have something to fire.

## Hints

There are no hints on the scoreboard and nothing costs points. Hints are spoken
to you on the day, and you earn them by sharing your screen.

**Share your screen in your team's Discord voice channel and you get two
hints**, one of them on a challenge we choose rather than one you pick. **Eden
is the exception: three hints on that one.**

We commentate the event live, and commentary needs something to watch. Watching
people actually work under pressure is the best part of the stream, so we pay
for it in hints.

## If your team name has an emoji or Arabic letters in it

Do not retype it. **Copy the team name straight out of the email we sent your
team leader** and paste it into the sign-in box. The name in that email is
exactly the one the scoreboard is expecting.

## Solving Android or Pwn

**Whoever solves SecureVault or Eden can join Rushd without sitting the Dar
al-Rushd entry CTF**, if they want to. That is the person who solved it, not the
whole team.

The rest of the team are welcome to join too, and are still asked to take the
entry CTF. We look at those one by one.

Challenges are independent. If you are stuck, leave it and come back. Nothing
is gated behind anything else.
