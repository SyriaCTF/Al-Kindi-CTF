# KindiSpider

**Forensics · 100 points**

A tier-1 analyst opened a case against a wave of business email compromise,
got halfway, and escalated it to you. Their scratchpad and everything they
pulled off the wire and off the host are in this folder.

Assemble the live campaign flag. **It comes in two halves.**

Retired and decoy values are in here on purpose. Every one of them tells you
what it is if you read the field it is sitting in, so slow down before you
submit something that looks right.


Entirely offline. Nothing to install beyond the usual forensics tooling, and
nothing here needs a paid licence.
